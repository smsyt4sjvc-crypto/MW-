The facility range tracks brownfield conversion, conventional greenfield construction, and greenfield with islanded generation and batteries. "All-in dollars per GW" is not a portable constant.

## Capacity-visibility rule

Track irreversible commitments rather than headline queue requests:

- supplier backlog with a stated delivery window;
- approved interconnection;
- equipment in a named delivery schedule;
- construction underway;
- financing closed with disclosed pricing;
- contracted offtake.

Announced GW, application queues, and target commercial-operation dates remain separate.

## Current forward calendar anchor

CAT Monarch's 2 GW of generators scheduled for delivery from September 2026 through August 2027 is the stored pre-registered capacity event. The useful project is a capacity-visibility calendar, not a hindsight-fitted semiconductor trading signal.
