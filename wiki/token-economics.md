# Token economics

## July 2026 direct Jevons observation

Vercel's production sample reported:

- token volume +59% month over month;
- spend +37%;
- realized dollars per token -13.6%;
- exact break-even volume growth +15.7%.

This is direct evidence that volume growth exceeded realized price deflation in that sample. It is not a global market census.

## September 2026 market split

The 2026-09-06 Vercel endpoint showed open-weight models at 65.3% of tokens. DeepSeek carried 41.3% of lab tokens and 4.7% of spend; Anthropic 11.5% and 54.8%; OpenAI 11.3% and 21.5%. Spend-share divided by token-share implies relative spend intensity near 0.11x, 4.77x, and 1.90x respectively.

The economic picture is a routing barbell: commodity models win volume while premium models retain disproportionate dollars. The ordinary consumer/app subscription market and high-volume routed production market must not be blended.

## Weekly four-dataset structure

1. Silicon Data: broad, open, proprietary realized expenditure and GPU rental indexes.
2. Vercel: token volume, spend, realized price, model mix, tool-call intensity.
3. OpenRouter: routed volume, model rankings, and market breadth.
4. Enterprise split: Anthropic Economic Index, OpenAI Signals, Ramp, and Menlo for consumer versus normal enterprise versus AI-intensive production use.

## Missing best unit

Spend per completed task remains the most useful missing revenue series. Raw tokens grow when tasks become longer, agents retry, tools call, context replays, or hidden reasoning expands. Those tokens are real hardware demand but not necessarily proportionate user value.
