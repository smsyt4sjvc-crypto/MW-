# Revenue per effective MW

## Current comparable anchors

| Economic form | Annual revenue per IT-load GW | Evidence class |
|---|---:|---|
| Merchant/spot compute | $10-12B | market/model estimate |
| Acceptable operator economics | $15B | external threshold; derivation open |
| Nscale/Anthropic | $16.3B | derived from a six-year contract |
| Healthy long-term case | $25B | aspiration/model case |

The Nscale/Anthropic derivation is $45B total contract value divided by six years and then by 0.460 GW of contracted Rubin capacity. It produces $7.5B annual contract revenue and $16.3B per IT-load GW-year.

## Why the company method matters

Cloud hyperscaler revenue mixes AI compute with storage, databases, software, support, and conventional workloads. Neocloud revenue may include networking and managed services. Meta primarily monetizes compute through advertising and engagement rather than selling GPU-hours. NVIDIA captures silicon and system content per GW rather than operator compute revenue.

The workbook therefore preserves separate numerators and methods while normalizing only the final unit.

## Forward test

NVIDIA's silicon-content ladder is $18B/GW Hopper, $25B/GW Blackwell, and $40B/GW Rubin. Rubin raises content per GW about 60% from Blackwell. Operator revenue/GW must rise comparably to hold gross-revenue payback constant.
