# Oracle OCI

Canonical company review: `research/companies/oracle_oci/2026-09-11.json`; method `SEGMENT-OCI`. The Project Jupiter credit event is documented separately in the public project dossier as of September 21, 2026.

## Current read

Oracle's OCI model remains partial. IaaS revenue is not AI-only, and period-average active IT MW, matched PUE and physical utilization remain undisclosed in the sources read. Capacity additions must not be used as the fleet denominator.

Oracle reported $125.337B of notes payable and other borrowings and $43.806B of recognized operating and finance lease liabilities at August 31, 2026. It also disclosed $288B of additional, mostly data-center lease commitments that had not yet commenced, plus $34.15B of unconditional purchase and other obligations primarily for cloud-infrastructure components and data-center power. Those commitments should not be added together as if they were one homogeneous debt balance.

The counterweight is $664B of remaining performance obligations and $19.909B of Q1 FY2027 at-the-market equity proceeds. Q1 operating cash flow included an $11.363B change in deferred revenues from customer prepayments with a significant financing component. Q1 capital expenditure was $28.499B and operating cash flow less capex was negative $5.396B. RPO is contracted backlog, not cash, recognized revenue or proof of economic return; customer prepayments are funding support and deferred revenue, not current recognized revenue.

## Project Jupiter

The roughly $18B financing tied to Project Jupiter is project debt arranged around developer/project-company borrowers and an Oracle tenancy. It is not established as $18B of direct Oracle corporate borrowing. Financial Times reporting placed private loan indications at 89–91 cents on the dollar on September 18, implying a mechanical $1.62B–$1.98B discount to face if applied to the entire commitment. That is a valuation indication—not a reported executed trade, realized loss, payment default, acceleration or recovery estimate.

The separate $165B industrial-revenue-bond figure is a 30-year authorization and tax/title structure ceiling. It is not evidence that $165B of cash debt has been funded. County reporting estimated $4.7B invested through June 30, with the data-center component about 9% complete and the microgrid about 10% complete.

The proposed onsite design is 2,462MW of generation capacity. Dividing the $18B loan by that figure gives about $7.31M per proposed generation MW, but this is only a debt/capacity intensity. Generation MW cannot be substituted for accepted IT-load MW or effective utilized IT MW.
