## Calculation

- Total contract value: $45B.
- Term: six years.
- Annual contract value: $7.5B.
- Contracted Rubin IT load: 460 MW.
- Annual contract revenue per IT-load MW: $16.3M.

The contract sits within a 1.35 GW campus reported at $71.2B all-in capex. Attributable project capex for 460 MW is about $24.2B, so the six-year contract covers attributable capex about 1.86 times before operating costs and financing.

## Unique method

This is a contract-rate benchmark, not realized utilization economics. Take-or-pay credit quality can support revenue even when physical utilization differs.
