# IREN

**Method:** `MERCHANT-IREN`  
**Status:** inputs open

## Numerator

Annualized AI cloud and services revenue only.

## Denominator

Average active AI IT MW multiplied by utilization.

## Unique method

Exclude Bitcoin-mining revenue and mining power completely. Power transferred from mining to AI becomes part of the AI denominator only when it is converted, commissioned, and active for AI workloads.

## Open

- AI-only revenue.
- Average active AI MW.
- Utilization and contract/merchant mix.
