# IREN

**Method:** `MERCHANT-IREN`  
**Status:** partial review through September 16, 2026

## Numerator

Q4 FY2026 AI Cloud Services revenue was **$70.515M**, derived from FY2026 less the first nine months. Annualized on a simple quarter-times-four convention, the numerator is **$282.06M**. Bitcoin-mining revenue is excluded.

## Denominator

The denominator remains open: the required measure is the 91-day average of active AI IT MW cohorts multiplied by physical utilization. IREN's 40MW June 30 operating-capacity disclosure is an exit value with an unlabeled power boundary, so it is not used.

## Unique method

Exclude Bitcoin-mining revenue and mining power completely. Power transferred from mining to AI becomes part of the AI denominator only when it is converted, commissioned, and active for AI workloads.

## Power and PUE

- Prince George and Mackenzie each report average PUE of **1.1** on current site pages.
- Neither page provides a measurement period, facility/IT energy totals or commissioning rules, and Q4 site IT-energy weights are absent.
- The 1.1 values are therefore named-site proxies, not a Q4 fleet PUE. The model retains Q4 fleet PUE as `not_disclosed`.

## Contract and financing economics

- The filed Microsoft contract implies **$9.67M per stated IT MW-year** from $9.6668B over 200 IT MW and an average five-year term. This is contract value, not recognized revenue or revenue per effective MW.
- Management reports recent three-year contracts above $20M per stated IT MW-year and active discussions around $25M, but those are forward claims rather than Q4 realized economics.
- At June 30, deferred lease revenue was **$1.62348B** while FY2026 lease revenue was zero. Customer cash cannot be treated as current revenue.
- The $3.645B Microsoft GPU financing is asset- and contract-secured. It limits general recourse but retains parent guarantees for hedges, remarketing shortfalls and managed-service performance.
