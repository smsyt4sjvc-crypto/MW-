## Forward test

The 60% Rubin/Blackwell content increase implies matching revenue growth only to preserve the same supplier-component capital/gross-revenue ratio at identical boundaries. Whole-project costs, financing, energy, utilization, replacement dates and recoveries determine cash payback. Token-cost benchmarks do not establish realized Jevons economics.

The current read is **financing-dependent supplier growth**. PORTS has a $105B contingent guarantee cap for 4.25 planned IT GW: $24.71M maximum credit support per planned IT MW, neither funded capital nor expected loss. Nine phases start, under current guidance, in FY2029. Obligations cover defined lease/power payments, decline over 20-year leases, and terminate on specified events. Optional expansion is excluded (`SRC-NVDA-10Q-Q2FY27`, Note10 pp18-19).

The additional $36B of AI-cloud service commitments can fall as capacity is resold or used; third-party revenue share is conditional. Keep these separate from $29B R&D cloud agreements, $20B of leases intended for reassignment and $25B internal uncommenced leases. The >$500B financing-platform announcement is preliminary MOUs, not funded NVIDIA debt; no universal 25% residual-value guarantee is established (`SRC-NVDA-FINANCE-MOU-20260810`).
