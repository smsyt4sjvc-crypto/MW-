# Microsoft Azure

**Method:** `SEGMENT-AZURE`  
**Status:** inputs open

## Numerator

Annualized Azure or Intelligent Cloud revenue attributable to AI compute. Do not use total Intelligent Cloud revenue without removing server products, support, databases, and non-AI workloads.

## Denominator

Weighted-average effective utilized Azure AI IT MW for the same period.

## Unique method

Use disclosed AI contribution to Azure growth as an attribution bridge only when the base and period are compatible. Keep first-party model workloads, OpenAI-related capacity, and conventional cloud workloads separate where the disclosure permits.

## Open

- Matching-period AI revenue attribution.
- Average active AI IT MW rather than announced campus capacity.
- Treatment of capacity committed to OpenAI but not yet active.
