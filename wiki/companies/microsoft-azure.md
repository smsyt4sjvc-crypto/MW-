# Microsoft Azure

**Method:** `SEGMENT-AZURE`  
**Status:** partial review through 2026-09-17

## Numerator

Microsoft's September 2, 2026 segment exhibit restates exact Azure revenue at $101.938 billion for FY2026 and $29.417 billion for Q4. The FY2027 definition includes cloud and AI consumption-based services and virtual desktop offerings, after moving GitHub and other developer cloud services, Security Copilot, and Healthcare and Life Sciences cloud out of Azure.

Use the Q4 Azure revenue only after sourcing the external AI-compute share. Do not substitute Intelligent Cloud, Microsoft Cloud, commercial RPO, OpenAI's $250 billion service commitment, customer prepayments, or external compute contract values for recognized Azure AI revenue.

## Denominator

Use the 91-day time-weighted average active Azure AI IT MW across owned, leased, colocated, and purchased external capacity, multiplied by physical workload utilization. A quarterly capacity addition, reserved/contracted capacity, PPA generation MW, and exit capacity are not period-average effective IT MW.

Microsoft said it added 1 GW of capacity in Q4 FY2026, but did not disclose the power boundary or activation dates. It is excluded from the denominator.

## PUE

Microsoft reports FY2025 global PUE of 1.17 for datacenters it fully owned and controlled that operated for all twelve months. Regional PUE was 1.16 Americas, 1.28 Asia Pacific, and 1.16 EMEA. The historical boundary excludes leased, colocated, commissioning, and new capacity, so it is not selected for the Q4 FY2026 Azure AI model.

The FY2025 environmental table lists 15,931,489 MWh of facility electricity for a related owned-and-controlled boundary. That equals 1,818.7 MW average facility load and 1,554.4 MW implied IT load at PUE 1.17. This is a coverage diagnostic only; the electricity and PUE boundaries are similar but not proven identical and neither matches total Azure AI revenue.

## Capital and contracts

- FY2026 cash property-and-equipment additions were $115.948 billion; finance-lease right-of-use additions were $24.608 billion. Their $140.556 billion sum is a capex-like measure, not Azure AI-only cash capex. Roughly two thirds of $41 billion Q4 capex was short-lived CPUs and GPUs, and server/network equipment retains a 2-6 year accounting life.
- June 2026 lease, purchase, and construction obligations totaled $672.132 billion, or 2.03 times FY2026 revenue. Purchase commitments are primarily datacenters and include open purchase orders and take-or-pay contracts; the disclosed buckets are not fully AI-attributed.
- OpenAI contracted an incremental $250 billion of Azure services. The April 2026 amendment retained Microsoft as primary cloud partner while allowing OpenAI products on other clouds and changing revenue-share and IP terms.
- Filed IREN and Nebius contracts document Microsoft purchasing external GPU/IT capacity with acceptance, delivery, prepayment, availability, and termination mechanics. These contracts do not establish Microsoft's current physical utilization.
- The 20-year Constellation PPA supports approximately 835 MW of generation to the PJM grid, expected online in 2028. It is not Azure IT MW or PUE.
