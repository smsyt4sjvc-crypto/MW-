# Nebius

**Method:** `MERCHANT-NBIS`  
**Status:** inputs open

## Numerator

Annualized AI infrastructure revenue.

## Denominator

Weighted-average connected active IT MW multiplied by utilization.

## Unique method

Separate active, reserved, contracted, construction, and planned capacity. A large unconcentrated customer tail may make merchant pricing more relevant than contracted anchor pricing.

## Open

- Active connected MW on the revenue period.
- Utilization and contract mix.
- Compute-only revenue scope.
