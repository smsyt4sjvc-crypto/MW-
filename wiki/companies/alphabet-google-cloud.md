# Alphabet Google Cloud

**Method:** `SEGMENT-GCP`  
**Status:** inputs open

## Numerator

Annualized external Google Cloud revenue attributable to AI compute.

## Denominator

Weighted-average effective utilized GCP AI IT MW.

## Unique method

Separate external GCP monetization from internal Search, YouTube, Gemini, and other first-party workloads. TPU ownership means operator and supplier economics partly collapse into one company, so NVIDIA-content benchmarks cannot be applied without an architecture adjustment.

## Open

- External AI revenue attribution.
- External versus internal workload power split.
- TPU versus third-party accelerator mix.
