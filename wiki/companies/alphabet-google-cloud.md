# Alphabet Google Cloud

**Method:** `SEGMENT-GCP`  
**Status:** partial review 2026-09-20; canonical output open

## Numerator

Annualized recognized external GCP AI compute service revenue. Q2 2026 Google Cloud revenue was $24.768 billion, but the segment includes GCP, Workspace, security/data services and TPU system sales. TPU product revenue and backlog stay outside the recurring compute-service numerator.

## Denominator

Time-weighted active external GCP AI IT MW by hosted TPU, hosted NVIDIA and purchased third-party capacity cohort, multiplied by matching physical utilization. Exit capacity, future joint-venture MW and chip counts without full-system power are excluded.

## Unique method

Separate external GCP monetization from internal Search, YouTube, Gemini and other first-party workloads. Management prioritizes TPU allocation to frontier-model development, sells TPU systems, and buys third-party capacity to bridge external demand. TPU ownership therefore collapses operator and supplier economics partly into one company; segment-average hardware assumptions are invalid.

Google reports fleet TTM PUE of 1.09 through Q2 2026 and quarterly PUE of 1.10. This is an operational fleet proxy, not a matched GCP AI PUE, so no PUE is selected in the model.
