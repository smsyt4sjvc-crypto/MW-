# Meta

**Method:** `INTERNAL-META`  
**Status:** inputs open

## Numerator

Incremental advertising, engagement, commerce, or external Meta Compute revenue attributable to AI compute.

## Denominator

Weighted-average effective utilized Meta AI IT MW supporting the attributed revenue.

## Unique method

Meta monetizes most compute internally. Total advertising revenue divided by AI MW is prohibited because legacy ad revenue predates the measured compute. Maintain two separate cases: internal incremental monetization and any external compute sales.

## Open

- Causal or management-provided incremental revenue bridge.
- External Meta Compute revenue and rented hardware vintage.
- Average AI IT MW by workload.
