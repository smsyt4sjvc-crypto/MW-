# Amazon AWS

**Method:** `SEGMENT-AWS`  
**Status:** inputs open

## Numerator

Annualized AWS revenue attributable to AI compute.

## Denominator

Weighted-average effective utilized AWS AI IT MW for the same period.

## Unique method

Separate first-party Trainium and Inferentia economics from rented NVIDIA/AMD capacity when possible. Retail and logistics capex never enters the power denominator or AI-compute capex numerator.

## Open

- AI-attributed AWS revenue.
- Average active AI IT MW.
- Architecture mix and utilization.
