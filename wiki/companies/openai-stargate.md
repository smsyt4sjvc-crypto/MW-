# OpenAI / Stargate

**Method:** `PROJECT-STARGATE`  
**Status:** partial primary model through 2026-10-07

## Current benchmark

Abilene's $15B project/JV funding and 1.2GW full-build facility design imply $12.5M per facility MW. Crusoe reports a 1.2-1.4 annualized **design** PUE, not measured operational PUE. That converts the full build to 857-1,000 IT MW and $15.0M-$17.5M of project capital per IT MW.

At explicit 70%-90% utilization sensitivities, project capital is $16.7M-$25.0M per effective utilized IT MW; base is $20.3M at 1.3 PUE and 80% utilization. This is capital intensity, not revenue productivity.

## Capacity timing

OpenAI says it has surpassed 10GW of secured/committed capacity, but that is not active or billable MW. As of June 9, 2026, Abilene's first two 100MW buildings were operational and six were still under construction. The model never divides revenue by the 1.2GW exit design or the 10GW commitment.

## Financing read

OpenAI announced $110B of new corporate investment, and SoftBank says its final tranche brought cumulative OpenAI investment to $64.6B. These amounts support the corporate balance sheet but are not project revenue or proof every site is fully financed.

Oracle's Q1 FY2027 filing reports $288B of uncommenced leases, substantially all data-center leases, plus $28.5B of quarterly capex and $11.4B of customer prepayments. These are Oracle-consolidated figures, not wholly OpenAI/Stargate.

Project Jupiter's county IRB authorization is a $165B ceiling rather than funded cash. NMED currently marks the microgrid air-permit process stayed by the New Mexico Supreme Court. FT separately reported about $18B of project loans indicated at 89-91 cents; that secondary report does not establish cancellation or default.
