# Broadcom

**Method:** `VENDOR-AVGO`  
**Status:** inputs partial

## Numerator

Custom accelerator and networking revenue attributable to a dated deployed-capacity schedule.

## Denominator

Deployed customer IT-load GW on the matching architecture and period.

## Stored anchor

The prior work derived roughly $11.5B of Broadcom custom-silicon content per IT-load GW. This is a single-vendor component of the compute stack, not total systems content.

## Unique method

Customer architecture mix determines Broadcom content. Do not apply the coefficient to an NVIDIA-dominant project or to server-system revenue without an explicit component bridge.
