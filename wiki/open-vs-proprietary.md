# Open-weight versus proprietary models

## Current interpretation

Open-weight models are disproportionately used in high-volume routed production workloads, not necessarily by the average enterprise or consumer. A small population of sophisticated customers can generate a large share of tokens while representing a much smaller share of spend.

Stored Ramp evidence placed model-serving-platform use at 5.8% of AI-spending businesses in June 2026, while those users spent roughly $248 per employee versus $10.59 for all AI-spending businesses, about 23x higher intensity. Most also used Anthropic or OpenAI. The emerging structure is multi-model routing, not a clean replacement from closed to open.

## August correction

Silicon Data's 2026-09-02 snapshot showed proprietary effective expenditure falling faster than open over 30 days: -25.4% versus -18.4%. That rejected the simple claim that only the cheap/open tier was deflating while frontier effective pricing held.

Because the indexes are usage weighted, the decline may reflect routing from flagship to cheaper proprietary tiers rather than direct list-price cuts. Track both index movement and published price changes.
